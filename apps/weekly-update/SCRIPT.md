# Pitch Video — Voiceover Script

**Total: 2 minutes (120s · 7200 frames @ 60fps)**

Delivery tips:
- Parle posé. ~150 mots/min — tu as de la marge.
- Respire entre chaque section (au passage du dot qui change).
- Les `—` sont des beats courts, pas des virgules.
- Les **bold** dans le script = emphase quand tu lis.

---

## Scene 1 — Intro

**`[0:00 → 0:08]` · 8 seconds · HUD dot 1**

> "Hi, I'm **Ramy**. I go by **Slashy**. And this is **Protocol 01** — a post-quantum privacy layer for Solana."

*(beat — let the name land)*

## Scene 2 — Cyber → Crypto → Web3

**`[0:08 → 0:30]` · 22 seconds · HUD dot 2**

> "I'm a **cybersecurity engineer**. Security work pulls you toward **cryptography** — and the frontier of cryptography today is **web3**. That's why I went full-time on hackathons. **Nine months**, no fallback, shipping real products on-chain."

*(The 3 nodes Security → Cryptography → Web3 appear as you say them — match your rhythm to the cards.)*

---

## Scene 3 — The Problem

**`[0:30 → 1:00]` · 30 seconds · HUD dot 3**

> "Three months ago I opened the Protocol 01 repo."

*(pause — the "I OPENED THE REPO" glitch card lands)*

> "Here's the problem. Every Solana transaction is **public by default**. Balances. Senders. Recipients. Spending patterns. All visible. Forever. On-chain. And **no developer has ready-made privacy tools** — every project that needs confidentiality rebuilds it from scratch."

---

## Scene 4 — What I Built

**`[1:00 → 1:25]` · 25 seconds · HUD dot 4**

> "Protocol 01 is the privacy layer that doesn't exist. A complete stack — **ZK-STARKs** over the Goldilocks field, a **custom on-chain FRI verifier**, hybrid **stealth addresses** with ML-KEM-768, and a **merchant SDK** that lets any wallet publish on-chain as a real service. Netflix, Spotify, YouTube, Disney Plus are already registered on devnet. **Anyone can subscribe privately today.**"

*(Say "Disney Plus" not "Disney+" — it reads more naturally aloud.)*

---

## Scene 5 — Quantum-Resistant

**`[1:25 → 1:33]` · 8 seconds · HUD dot 5**

> "**No trusted setup. No ceremony. No zkey files.** Because Groth16 doesn't survive the quantum transition."

*(Hit the three "no" hard — this is the strongest punch-line of the pitch.)*

---

## Scene 6 — Why Me

**`[1:33 → 1:50]` · 17 seconds · HUD dot 6**

> "Why me? Because I'm **cybersecurity first, crypto builder second**. Fourteen Anchor programs, twelve SDKs, three clients, a P2P exchange — all in **three months**, solo, on zero funding. Not because I'm faster than teams. Because **I own every layer** — from the circuit up to the mobile UX. No handoff. Just me — and that **compresses the feedback loop to hours**."

---

## Scene 7 — Outro

**`[1:50 → 2:00]` · 10 seconds · HUD dot 7**

> "Privacy on Solana isn't a feature. It's a prerequisite for the next billion users who don't want their financial life indexed. **Protocol 01 is live on devnet. The repo is open.** Thanks."

*(Let "Thanks." fall gently — don't rush the last word. The outro glow animation takes a second to breathe.)*
