·Why this brief exists

The technical work is no longer the bottleneck. The product exists, the audit posture is good, two hackathon wins are on the board, and partnerships are starting to land. What is missing is someone who treats the story, the audience, and the channel mix with the same care and intensity that the codebase already gets.

Marketing, content, social, PR, narrative for investors and partners: these need a dedicated owner. Doing them on the side while shipping ZK circuits is no longer viable, and outsourcing them to an agency would produce the kind of generic crypto-content the protocol explicitly does not want. The right answer is a co-founder.

Built solo since end of January 2026 by one engineer. That same engineer is writing this brief.

You own the story, the audience, and every external touchpoint short of the actual business deals and the actual fundraise.

Brand & narrative

The story we tell about why a privacy layer matters on Solana, and why Protocol 01 is the credible one. Positioning, voice, message hierarchy.

Long-form, threads, demos. Every release, every circuit, every program goes out with intent.

A real X account, not a placeholder. A real cadence, not bursts around hackathons. Telegram, Discord, Superteam channels.

PR & podcasts

The right outlets, the right hosts, the right Twitter mutuals. The public face of the protocol when the founder cannot be in two places at once.

Audience growth

Numbers that go up, on the channels that matter. You define which channels matter and why, against owned and earned targets.

What is not in scope

AreaWhy the founder keeps it
Business development The founder handles partnerships, B2B SDK deals, ecosystem relationships (Solana Foundation, Superteam, Helius, Privy, Arcium, MagicBlock). You may surface leads and join meetings; you do not negotiate.

Section 03 · Prerequisites

03Hard requirements, in order

None of the items below are negotiable. Each one has closed a door for us at least once already.

01 · Crypto-native

You already use Solana, hold a wallet, have an opinion on at least three existing privacy projects, and understand the difference between a SNARK and a STARK well enough to brief a journalist. You do not need to write circuits. You do need to follow them without translation.

02 · Fluency with ZK and privacy primitives

You can explain in plain language what a zero-knowledge proof is, what a SNARK versus STARK trade-off looks like, why post-quantum matters for signatures and for hashes, what a stealth address protects (and what it does not), what Arcium-style MPC adds on top of pure ZK, and where ML-KEM-768 fits in our stealth pipeline. You can read a Poseidon, Merkle, or commitment-scheme reference without losing the room.

AztecPenumbraAleoNocturneTornadoRailgunLightElusivUmbraArcium

Familiarity with any of these is the baseline. Familiarity with none is a hard stop. Every founder, every investor, every podcast host in this niche tests you on this in the first ninety seconds.

03 · Solana / Ireland network, real and live

Superteam IE is the obvious starting point, but the bar is higher than "I am in the Telegram". You have shipped something, organised something, written something, or onboarded someone here, and people answer when you DM them.

04 · Demonstrable crypto comms output

Not "I ran ads for a SaaS in 2022". Threads that performed, content that travelled, a project whose narrative you can take partial credit for, a community you grew, a launch you led. We will ask to see it.

05 · Motion design & video editing

This is a hard requirement, not a bonus. You can take a brief and ship a finished 60-second cut on your own: timeline, motion, sound, captions, export. Tooling is open (After Effects, Premiere, DaVinci, Final Cut, Remotion if you code, anything else that ships) but the output bar is high. Bring a reel.

Non-crypto reel that is visually sharp works. Crypto reel that is slow and templated does not.

06 · Excellent written English

The narrative for Protocol 01 is currently in two languages and needs to consolidate, sharpen, and travel. You will write a lot.

07 · Comfort with the founder's working style

Solo for four months, async days, plain prose over emoji-heavy decks, low tolerance for marketing clichés ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "unlocks").

Section 06 · Process

06Three steps, then a one-week micro-engagement

Designed to respect everyone's time. No deck or full CV asked at step one.

Step 01 · Written exchange

Send a short note plus three artefacts:

  • One link to a piece of comms work you are proud of.
  • One link to a piece of motion or video work you are proud of.
  • One paragraph: your take on what Protocol 01's positioning should be.

Step 02 · Video call with the founder

Forty-five minutes. We talk about the protocol, about your work, about how we would split decisions. No prepared questions, just a real conversation.

Step 03 · Equity-aligned micro-engagement

One week, one deliverable (a short positioning document or a thread on a current release). Counts toward the trial's 2% advisor grant. Both sides validate the working rhythm before signing the 90-day trial.

If all three go well, we sign the 90-day trial agreement and start within two weeks.

What to send, and where

Reply via the Superteam Ireland channel Diarmuid forwarded along with this document. Subject line: "Protocol 01 co-founder · [your name]". The founder writes back personally.

If you are unsure whether to apply

Apply anyway. The two hardest filters in this brief are crypto-nativeness and motion-design output. If you are confident on those two, the rest is conversation. If you are uncertain on one, say so up front in your note. We would rather know.

Thank you for reading

This brief is intentionally short. If a question is missing here, ask it in your first note.
