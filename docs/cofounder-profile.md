# Co-founder profile — Marketing & Communications

A short brief for Superteam Ireland, written in the same plain style we use for the rest of Protocol 01.

## What Protocol 01 is, today

Protocol 01 is the privacy layer Solana doesn't have yet. Post-quantum ZK-STARKs (Winterfell, Poseidon over Goldilocks), stealth addresses (X25519 plus ML-KEM-768), on-chain verification. Twelve Solana programs, six circuits, a STARK prover compiled to WASM, three client apps (mobile on Expo 54, a Chrome extension, a Next.js 16 web app), and a growing family of SDKs (zk-sdk, zkspl-sdk, specter-sdk, p01-js, arcium-sdk, plus a privacy-toolkit and react-native-zk).

The stack is live on devnet. Shield, unshield, transfer, confidential balances, denominated pools, and Merkle update are all covered by STARK circuits that verify on-chain. The hardening pass is well underway: V4 pools migrated after a legacy event bug, relayer hosted on Railway, event scrub deployed, refund pipeline shipped. A separate product, Mugen, layers P2P fiat-to-crypto with no KYC on top of the same primitives.

It has been built solo since end of January 2026 by one engineer. That same engineer is writing this brief.

## Why this brief exists

The technical work is no longer the bottleneck. The product exists, the audit posture is good, two hackathon wins are on the board (Dev3pack #2 worldwide on the Solana track, Colosseum submitted), and partnerships are starting to land. What is missing is someone who treats the story, the audience, and the channel mix with the same care and intensity that the codebase already gets.

Marketing, content, social, PR, narrative for investors and partners: these need a dedicated owner. Doing them on the side while shipping ZK circuits is no longer viable, and outsourcing them to an agency would produce the kind of generic crypto-content the protocol explicitly does not want. The right answer is a co-founder.

This document spells out what that co-founder looks like and what they would walk into.

## The role, in one sentence

Co-founder, Head of Marketing & Communications. You own the story, the audience, and every external touchpoint short of the actual business deals and the actual fundraise.

## What is in scope

You own marketing strategy and execution end to end. That means brand and narrative (the story we tell about why a privacy layer matters on Solana, and why Protocol 01 is the credible one), content across formats (long-form, threads, demos), social presence (a real X account, not a placeholder; a real cadence, not bursts around hackathons), launches (every release, every circuit, every program goes out with intent), and community (Telegram, Discord, Superteam channels, the right rooms at conferences).

You own video and motion content. Protocol 01 already has an internal Remotion-based pipeline for 4K renders (weekly updates, pitch films, demo footage). You take that pipeline over and you ship at cadence: weekly updates that travel, release videos for each circuit, conference cuts, vertical clips for X and YouTube Shorts, animated explainers for ZK and stealth primitives that a non-engineer can follow. This is not a "nice if you find someone later" line. It is a core part of how Protocol 01 differentiates from the wall of static threads in the privacy space.

You own communications. That means PR (the right podcasts, the right press, the right Twitter mutuals), positioning (we are not "another mixer", we are not "another L2", we are the privacy layer Solana doesn't have yet, and the wording matters), and the public face of the protocol in places where the founder cannot be in two places at once.

You own audience growth. Numbers that go up, on the channels that matter. You define which channels matter and why.

You partner closely on product narrative. When a circuit ships, when a program is deployed, when a feature changes, you are in the loop early enough to shape how it lands. You will not be handed a finished release and asked to "post about it on Tuesday".

## What is not in scope

You do not own business development. The founder handles partnerships, B2B SDK deals, ecosystem relationships (Solana Foundation, Superteam, Helius, Privy, Arcium, MagicBlock), and any conversation that ends in a contract. You may surface leads, attend meetings, and help with materials, but you are not negotiating.

You do not own fundraising. The founder leads conversations with investors, owns the cap table, signs term sheets. You will help build the deck, the data room narrative, the public artefacts that warm up a round, but the round itself is not your domain.

You do not own engineering, product, or infrastructure. The technical roadmap, the protocol design, the security posture, the release cadence are decided by the founder.

Drawing this line clearly up front is intentional. The asymmetry it creates is reflected in the equity terms below.

## Prerequisites

Crypto-native is non-negotiable. You should already use Solana, hold a wallet, have an opinion on at least three existing privacy projects, and understand the difference between a SNARK and a STARK well enough to brief a journalist. You do not need to write circuits. You do need to follow them without translation.

Fluency with the ZK and privacy primitives Protocol 01 ships. Concretely: you can explain in plain language what a zero-knowledge proof is, what a SNARK versus a STARK trade-off looks like, why post-quantum matters for signatures and for hashes, what a stealth address protects (and what it does not), what Arcium-style MPC adds on top of pure ZK, and where ML-KEM-768 fits in our stealth pipeline. You should also be able to read a Poseidon, Merkle, or commitment-scheme reference without losing the room. If you have followed Aztec, Penumbra, Aleo, Nocturne, Tornado, Railgun, Light, Elusiv, Umbra, or Arcium closely, that is the baseline. Not knowing any of them is a hard stop. The reason is simple: every founder, every investor, every podcast host who matters in this niche will test you on this in the first ninety seconds, and a vague answer will close doors that took the founder twelve months of code to open.

A live network in the Solana ecosystem, ideally with strong roots in Ireland or wider Europe. Superteam IE is the obvious starting point, but the bar is higher than "I am in the Telegram". You have shipped something, organised something, written something, or onboarded someone here, and people answer when you DM them.

Demonstrable marketing or comms output in crypto. Not "I ran ads for a SaaS in 2022". Threads that performed, content that travelled, a project whose narrative you can take partial credit for, a community you grew, a launch you led. We will ask to see it.

Motion design and video editing chops. This is a hard requirement, not a bonus. You can take a brief and ship a finished 60-second cut on your own: timeline, motion, sound, captions, export. Tooling is open (After Effects, Premiere, DaVinci, Final Cut, Remotion if you code, anything else that ships) but the output bar is high. Bring a reel. If your existing reel is non-crypto but visually sharp, that works; if it is crypto but slow and templated, that does not.

Excellent written English. The narrative for Protocol 01 is currently in two languages and needs to consolidate, sharpen, and travel. You will write a lot.

Comfort with the founder's working style. Solo for four months, deep technical sessions, asynchronous days, a strong preference for plain prose over emoji-heavy decks, and a low tolerance for marketing clichés ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "unlocks") in anything we publish.

## Nice to have

Prior co-founder experience, even at a smaller scale. You know what the first eighteen months feel like, you know how to disagree with a co-founder without escalating, and you have seen at least one fundraise from inside.

A track record at a previous privacy or infrastructure project on Solana, Ethereum, Cosmos, or Polkadot. Bonus if you have lived through the gap between "the protocol works" and "the protocol is understood".

Code-aware motion work. Familiarity with Remotion or any code-first motion stack (Manim, GSAP, Lottie pipelines) lets you plug straight into our existing render pipeline instead of rebuilding it in a GUI tool. The current pipeline already does chunked GPU 4K renders with FFmpeg concat, and someone who can extend it directly will move twice as fast.

Spoken French. Not required. Useful, given where the founder is based.

Video, podcast, or on-stage comfort. We will need a public face that is not only the founder, and we will need it sooner than later.

## Commitment and terms

The proposal is an equity-only 90-day trial followed by full-time co-founder status. Both sides need an honest exit ramp before the larger equity grant is on the table, and a single product is at risk if either side gets locked in too early.

During the trial, you work part-time (10 to 15 hours per week) on a small set of agreed deliverables: a six-month communications plan, a refreshed narrative and positioning document, a first cycle of outbound (podcasts, PR, thought-leadership), and a relaunch of the public X presence. There is no cash compensation during the trial. The trial is equity-only: a 2% advisor grant, cliffed at successful 90-day completion. If the trial does not convert, the 2% advisor grant remains acquired.

If both sides confirm the fit at day 90, you become full-time co-founder. Equity is 27% of common stock, four-year vesting, one-year cliff, monthly thereafter. The 2% advisor grant from the trial remains acquired in addition to the 27% base. The vesting clock backdates to day one of the trial as an acknowledgement of the work done in the trial period, but only the founder-confirmed full-time start unlocks the cliff.

Beyond the 27% base, up to 9% in additional equity is available against performance milestones to be agreed in writing before full-time start. Examples (non-binding, to be tailored to the candidate): audience growth targets on owned channels, named press placements in tier-one outlets, contribution to a closed funding round, B2B SDK deals where comms materially shortened the cycle. Total equity is capped at 38% in all scenarios. The founder retains 62% minimum.

There is no cash compensation during the pre-funding phase. This is an equity-only co-founder path. A market-rate salary floor is written into the full-time contract, triggered once a funding round closes. We will talk numbers in person, not in a brief.

The role is remote-first. A physical presence in or near Dublin is a positive factor, not a requirement.

## What you are walking into

Honest version, because that is the only kind worth reading.

The protocol is technically very advanced for a four-month-old solo effort. It has also burned through one person's personal runway, and we are entering a fundraising phase that is not yet certain to land. You are betting on a strong product in a hard category, not on a Series A track record.

Two recent signals matter: a second place finish out of hundreds of teams at the Dev3pack global hackathon on the Solana track, and a Colosseum Frontier submission with a credible accelerator track application. Neither pays the rent, both move the needle.

The codebase is in a good place but the public face of the project is not. There is no real X presence, no content cadence, no narrative document anyone outside the founder's head can read. The first 60 days of work are obvious. You will not be inventing problems to solve.

The founder publishes as Slashy under the Volta Team handle (X, GitHub, Superteam). He is direct, technical, French, and currently based in Ireland. He will give you ownership of your domain and expect you to use it. He will also push back hard when the comms output drifts toward clichés or generic crypto-Twitter performance theatre. Both of those are features, not bugs.

## Process

Three steps, designed to respect everyone's time.

First, a written exchange. Send a short note, a link to one piece of comms work you are proud of, a link to one piece of motion or video work you are proud of, and a one-paragraph take on what you think Protocol 01's positioning should be. No deck, no CV needed yet.

Second, a video call with the founder. Forty-five minutes. We talk about the protocol, about your work, about how we would split decisions. No prepared questions, just a real conversation.

Third, an equity-aligned micro-engagement before the formal trial: one week, one deliverable (a short positioning document or a thread on a current release). The work counts toward the trial's 2% advisor grant. This protects both sides against a 90-day commitment based on vibes alone.

If all three go well, we sign the trial agreement and start day one within two weeks.
