Soundness, measured

The honest figure is in the forties of bits at best.

Soundness is derived per circuit from the deployed configuration by tools/security-levels, not quoted from a formula. Each range below is the floor of docs/SECURITY-LEVELS.md for the verifier as shipped, which draws each challenge as a machine word reduced mod p, next to its regime.

Why more queries buy little

Every Fiat‑Shamir challenge is a single Goldilocks element, so none carries more than 64 bits. The conjectured column is floor bound by the field term, (8n + w + k + 1 + folds × lde) over the field size, and its query term is 110 to 130 bits and is cut down to it. Grinding cannot lift the floor either: the nonce is absorbed last.

What the unique‑decoding column rests on

There the query term is the smaller quantity, so it binds and the 22 bits of grinding are load bearing. The rate is enforced, not assumed from the blowup of 16: a terminal polynomial is rejected above its degree bound, 1 of 16 coefficients on circuits 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 and 2 of 32 coefficients on circuits 0, 4 and 7, giving rho = 1/16 on every circuit, that is 4.000 bits per query across 22 or 27 queries.

This is not a production security level

At 10 GH/s of SHA‑256, 2^42 is about 7.3 GPU‑minutes and 2^46 about 2 GPU‑hours. The verifier's own test file, tests/b1_deep_binding.rs, states that nothing here is publishable as a security level. Nor does anything inside a proof bind which circuit produced it: the program stores no verification key hash, so circuit separation rests on one byte in the proof buffer account. Queries times log2(blowup) reached 104 to 124 bits, which the project no longer stands behind. tools/security‑levels prints every figure above from the verifier's own constants.

What raising it takes

Engineering, not research. The unique‑decoding column moves with the query count. The conjectured one is floor bound by the 64‑bit challenge, so a wider challenge is a design direction, not a result. Both cost compute: the last accepted circuit‑7 verification used 1,083,301 units in one transaction (phase 1 889,882, phase 2 193,269; blob 241caaab, slot 502,692,190) of the 1,400,000 a transaction allows, and that ceiling is fixed.
