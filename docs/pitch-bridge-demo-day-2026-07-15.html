01 Problem

Every payment on Solana is public.

Salaries, invoices, subscriptions, treasury moves. Readable by anyone, forever.

Pay a contractor

Their entire income history becomes public the moment you send. So does yours.

Subscribe to a service

Your subscriptions, habits and balance are one address lookup away for any scraper.

Run a business on-chain

Competitors read your revenue, your customer list and your margins, live.

This is the real blocker for on-chain payments. Businesses do not move payment volume onto a rail where every customer and every margin is public. Not speed. Not fees. Exposure.
PROTOCOL 0102 / 13
02 Solution

One rail. Two modes.

Every payment flow, P2P transfers, subscriptions and merchant checkout, exists in both modes on the same rail.

Classic mode

Transparent, fast, cheap. Normal Solana. Nothing to learn, nothing to migrate.

Private mode

Shielded and unlinkable, one tap. Proofs are generated on the user's phone. Nothing personal ever leaves the device.

What the subscriber gains

Identity, balance and payment history stay theirs. Subscribe to anything without broadcasting it to the chain.

What the merchant gains

Clean, final settlement with no chargebacks, and a subscriber who stays. Subscriptions acquire the merchant. Private balances retain the customer.

04 Under the hood

Post-quantum plumbing. Boring on purpose.

ZK-STARKs on device

Hash-based proofs with no trusted setup, generated on the phone. No ceremony, no toxic waste, no proving server reading your data.

Native on-chain verifier

A custom multi-circuit FRI verifier deployed on Solana checks proofs in under 1.4M CU.

Post-quantum addresses

Stealth addresses combine X25519 with ML-KEM-768 (NIST PQC), plus a WOTS+ hash-based vault as an Ed25519 hedge.

Relayer network

An on-chain relayer program with multi-relayer failover submits transactions, breaking transaction-graph links.

06 Business model

The fee rail is the business.

Protocol fee

Every private settlement pays an automatic on-chain fee of 0.3 to 0.5%. One fee rail across P2P, subscriptions and checkout. Collected at settlement by the program, no invoicing, no leakage.

Merchant SDK tiers

Standard and ZK license tiers with anonymous license keys, already live. Merchants integrate in days and never touch customer PII.

The wedge

Subscriptions acquire merchants. Recurring revenue is why they integrate. Private balances are why their subscribers stay.

Pre-revenue today, metered from day one. The fee logic is already deployed in the programs. Revenue switches on with mainnet, not with a business-model pivot.
PROTOCOL 0107 / 13
07 Why now

Payments moved on-chain. Privacy did not.

Stablecoins won

Stablecoins settle trillions per year and card networks are integrating them. Payments are the first crypto use case with real volume, and Solana is where they settle cheapest.

Solana validated the need

Solana shipped native Subscriptions and Allowances plus Confidential Transfers on mainnet in June 2026. Recurring is native now, amounts can be hidden, but who pays whom stays public. The ecosystem confirmed demand and left the hard part open.

The quantum clock is set

NIST guidance deprecates today's elliptic-curve crypto by 2030 to 2035. Everything public now can be harvested and read later. A payment rail built in 2026 should not expire in 2030.

Appendix Q&A backup

Likely questions, short answers.

Don't native Subscriptions and Allowances solve this?

They make recurring billing native, and Token-2022 can hide amounts. The delegation account still publicly links payer to merchant, forever. We hide the relationship itself, plus identity and balance.

Why is mainnet not live?

An external audit gates it, deliberately. Internal audits found real issues; fixes are shipped, validated on device and public. Funding the audit is part of this ask.

Compliance posture?

Merchants receive clean, attributable settlement; it is the user's graph that is protected. ZK innocence-proof selective disclosure is prototyped and on the roadmap.

Revenue today?

Zero. The 0.3 to 0.5% fee rail is already deployed in the programs, so monetization switches on with mainnet, not with a pivot.

Why post-quantum now?

Public data is harvested today and decrypted later. Hash-based proofs cost users nothing extra now and remove the migration cliff later.

Solo-founder risk?

Acknowledged and priced in: public repo, CI, tests, documented architecture, and an explicit cofounder search through this program.
