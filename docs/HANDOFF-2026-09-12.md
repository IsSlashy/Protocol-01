## 3. `[PERFECT-IOP 2026-09-12]` — the out-of-domain point is resampled

Why: the founder asked for "ZK parfait". Perfect zero-knowledge of the FULL
proof string is not reachable for any hash-based STARK (a Merkle root is a
deterministic function of 8,192 leaves with far fewer uniform degrees of
freedom; only Pedersen-style commitments are perfectly hiding, and those are
not post-quantum). What IS reachable, and is now written: the verifier's view
at the IOP level is simulated with an **identical** distribution — the one
failure event of `docs/zk-simulation-argument.md` (`S` fails with probability
~2^-54) was `z` landing in the trace domain (`Z_T(z) = 0`) or on the LDE coset
(a DEEP denominator vanishes). Both sides now reject such a candidate and
re-hash the 32 bytes:

- prover `stark/src/compact.rs`: `derive_ood_point_generic(.., trace_length, lde_size)`,
  `pub fn ood_point_from_hash`, `pub fn ood_point_from_transcript_hash`, and
  `mod ood_resampling_tests` (3 tests). The legacy `derive_ood_point` is unchanged
  (retired C0 shape, positive control).
- verifier `programs/p01_stark_verifier/src/verify.rs`: `derive_ood_point_generic(.., config)`
  called from `verify_generic` step 1b; same two `pub fn`s; legacy fn kept.
- new `programs/p01_stark_verifier/tests/ood_resampling_parity.rs` (2 tests):
  both crates agree on forced-bad and random candidates for all eight geometries.
- CU cost: two `exp`s per candidate; a second candidate is needed once in ~2^51 proofs.
- ⛔ This is a wire-rule change (the verifier recomputes `z`): it ships only
  with the verifier redeploy that the masked circuits already need. Do not
  reship the wasm blob before that redeploy. TS twins do not derive `z`.
- After it runs green: `docs/zk-simulation-argument.md` lines 54, 116, 344 say
  "fails with probability about 2^-54" — rewrite to "the simulated view is
  identically distributed; the residual is the random-oracle step on unopened
  leaves, bounded by `q_H · 2^-(w·63)` and measured by X5". Keep the
  "statistical, random-oracle" qualifiers in every public sentence.

## 4. X5 — unopened leaves conditioned on every published value

`stark/src/compact/zk_hiding.rs`, appended:
`unopened_leaves_stay_uniform_given_every_opening_on_every_circuit` (+
`batch_inv`, `lagrange_row_slopes`, `spread_queries_for`). For each circuit it
builds the span of the published functionals of a column (`z`, `z·g`, four
rows per opened pair, 27 queries ≥ every shipped count) over the free rows and
